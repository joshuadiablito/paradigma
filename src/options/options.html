Translation limit

Enter an email address like name@example.com, or leave it empty.

Free machine translation from MyMemory is limited to about 5,000 characters a day. Giving an email address raises that to 50,000. It's optional. It's sent only to MyMemory, with each translation request, and stored in your Chrome settings. Clear the field to remove it.

Site rules

Normally the page language is detected automatically. Rules override that for a site; add them from the toolbar button while you're on the site.
